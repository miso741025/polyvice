# Recordings of real places

The game makes all of its own sound, but a synthesiser is a poor substitute for a street. Any file put in this folder
under one of the names below is used instead of the synthesised layer it replaces. Any audio format the browser plays
will do (`.mp3`, `.m4a`, `.aac`, `.ogg`, `.wav`); the name before the dot is what matters. Loops of one to three
minutes are plenty. Nothing here is committed to git.

Start with the first four. Each replaces what is listed beside it.

| File name | What it should be | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| `vice-day` | Vice City street by day: a warm coastal city, light traffic, people, palms | city hum, birds, far-off sounds |
| `vice-night` | Vice City street at night: quieter, insects, distant traffic | city hum, crickets |
| `la-day` | Los Angeles street by day: big-city traffic wash, a freeway somewhere | city hum, birds, far-off sounds |
| `la-night` | Los Angeles at night: low city rumble, distant sirens | city hum, crickets |
| `beach` | Waves on sand, gulls | the sea, gulls (heard near the east shore, the pier, the bridge) |
| `car` | A car engine at a steady middling speed, from inside. It is sped up and slowed down with the revs | the engine |
| `room-bar` | A bar's crowd, without music | murmur and glasses in bars |
| `room-diner` | A diner or restaurant: voices, plates | murmur, cutlery, the grill |
| `room-hall` | A lobby or large room: quiet voices, footsteps | murmur in the bank, hotel, school, Green Grove |
| `room-store` | A small shop: a fridge, a radio somewhere | the hum in shops |
| `room-church` | An empty church | the drone |
| `room-garage` | A workshop: tools, a compressor | clanks and hum |
| `room-house` | A quiet house: a clock, a fridge | clock and hum |
| `suburb-day` | A quiet street of houses by day: birds, a lawnmower, a dog | used in place of the city file where there are houses and parks |
| `suburb-night` | The same at night: crickets | as above |
