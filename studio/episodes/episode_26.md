# Episode 26: Walking Papers

## Arc
Episode 25 ended with Aldo Ferro saying "Lyle walks": Lyle Teague, who skimmed Aldo's money and sold Agent Pembrook what he heard, is out of a job. A man with no job, no friends and a head full of Nico's week has one buyer left, and it is the government.
Enzo, who wants to be made, decides to sort Lyle out himself with a tyre iron. Nico gets there first, and now has to choose what to do with a man who has nothing left to lose: pay him to leave town, or give him work where he can be watched.
A tail to the pawnbroker's, a race to the bar and a chase on foot, Dr. Vos, an envelope slipped under a motel door past Pembrook, a ride with a brown sedan behind it, and a table at the Pelican where Enzo finds out who gets the credit. At home, Gina has found the bank book.

## Story reasons
- Pawned: a man who pawns his watch sells the next thing he owns, which is what he knows. Nico follows before he acts, because he needs to know how broke Lyle is.
- Enzo's Idea: the nephew who wants to be made tries to earn it alone. The clock is the time it takes Enzo to work up his nerve; the chase is Lyle, who has seen what Enzo is holding.
- Severance: family vs business at Dr. Vos's: the clean, expensive answer (a ticket) or the cheap, risky one (a job, and a man kept close).
- Under the Door: the fed has lost his source and is waiting for him to come back. The envelope is Nico's offer, and Pembrook must never know it came from Nico.
- One Way: Pembrook does not want his source to leave town or go to work for Nico, so he follows. The clock is the last bus, or the night shift.
- Walking Papers: Sergio settles the business (who pays, what Aldo hears); Enzo's credit and Gina's bank book settle the family.
