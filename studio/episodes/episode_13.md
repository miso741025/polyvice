# Episode Thirteen: The Return Address

## Arc
Episode Twelve ended with Nico driving Joey Finelli over the bridge himself, on Sergio's word that if Joey was on this side tomorrow, Sergio never had a driver. Enzo drives Sergio now.
Two weeks later a postcard comes to the Pelican with a Port Paloma postmark. Joey is back, and Agent Pembrook is buying him coffee. If Joey talks, the first name he gives is the man who drove him out.
Joey came back for a notebook: fourteen years of every stop he drove Sergio to, still taped under the seat of the grey sedan that Enzo drives now. Nico gets it before the F.B.I. does, puts Joey on the night truck out, and Gina finds the agent's card in the mailbox.

## Story reasons
- The postcard: Joey wants somebody to know he is back, and picks the one man who has to keep it quiet. Asking around is the only way to find him without asking Sergio.
- The stake-out and the tail: Nico cannot do anything about Joey until he knows who Joey is talking to. Following the agent to the luncheonette shows him the agent wants Sergio, not Joey.
- The therapy choice: the question of the series, whether Nico protects the boy by keeping him out of things or by teaching him. It decides whether Enzo is there when Joey sees them coming.
- The talk in the lot: Joey is a driver who knows he is nobody without the notebook. The calm, respectful choices are the ones that let him give it up.
- The heist: the notebook is in the car Enzo now drives. Getting it out from under the boy's nose is keeping him clean of it, which is what Nico did on the bridge.
- The night truck and Gina: Joey leaves the way Sergio meant him to the first time. The card in the mailbox is the wife's doubt made into an object: the F.B.I. knows where they live.
