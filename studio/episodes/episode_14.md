# Episode Fourteen: Anytime

## Arc
Episode Thirteen ended with the notebook burning in the sink and Gina keeping Agent Pembrook's card, the one with "Anytime" written on the back.
Pembrook knew about Wednesday at the luncheonette and about the night truck. Somebody close is talking, and Enzo has heard that the man who finds the rat gets his button.
It is not a man. It is the telephone behind the bar at the Pelican. Nico feeds the wire a box of cannoli, and Gina gives the card back to Pembrook herself.

## Story reasons
- The questions: Nico cannot ask Sergio who is talking without making Sergio wonder about Nico. Asking the crew where they said it is safer, and every answer points at the same telephone. Nico hears "Petey" when he should hear "the phone".
- The tail: Petey is the easy answer, and Nico wants an easy answer. Following him shows Nico his own mistake: Petey's secret is a nurse.
- The therapy choice: the episode's question is whether the threat is a person or the walls. Bringing Enzo in is giving the boy what he wants, a man to hurt, which is the wrong answer for both of them.
- The chase: if Enzo takes Petey to the marsh, an innocent man gets hurt so the boy can be made. Ramming the coupe is how Nico keeps both of them clean.
- The search: Petey says it himself: "it is always in the last place you look". Unscrewing the phone, slowly, is the proof that nobody in the crew talked.
- The cannoli: a mob boss cannot pull a wire without telling the F.B.I. he found it. He can make them look foolish on it. The photograph is the proof the line is theirs.
- Gina: she kept the card in Episode Thirteen, and Nico has been afraid all episode of what she might say. The card going back across a café table is the wife's doubt answered in her own way. She did it for the house, not for him, and the kitchen talk is where he has to accept that.
