# Episode Twelve: The Nice Tie

## Arc
Episode Eleven ended with Lyle Teague naming a man in a nice tie who left the Funland booth on the latch, and Sergio deciding he now has two nephews to watch.
The nice tie is Joey Finelli, Sergio's driver. Nico needs proof he can put on his uncle's table before Enzo, three days in the book, settles it with a gun.
A photograph, a darkroom, a Thursday and a sit-down later, Sergio gives Joey up, and Nico drives him over the bridge himself so the boy never has to.

## Story reasons
- The tail and the picture: Nico cannot accuse his uncle's right hand on Teague's word. A picture is the one thing Sergio cannot call a nephew's grudge.
- The chase: Enzo's first act as a made man would be killing his boss's driver. Nico runs him down with his fists because a shot would end both of them.
- Gina: the gun in the hall, where Benny hangs his coat, is the wife's question for the whole series: which Nico comes home tomorrow.
- The darkroom: Joey has been on Nico since Lunch (Episode Ten). Getting the film past him is getting the proof past the man it proves. Rudy owes Nico his camera.
- The therapy choice: the only decision in the episode that is about Sergio's pride, so it is the one that changes the sit-down.
- The sit-down: Sergio has to give Joey up without being seen to lose. The calm, respectful choices are the ones that let him.
- The bridge: Sergio makes Nico do it himself, as a lesson. Enzo following is the boy not trusting the lesson. Losing him is how Nico keeps him clean.
