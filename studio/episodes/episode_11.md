# Episode Eleven: A Name in the Book

## Arc
Episode Ten ended with Sergio letting Nico think the back doctor had fooled him. He stops asking about Thursdays and starts being kind to Enzo instead.
He offers Enzo his name in the book for two things: a job against Aldo Ferro's boardwalk money, and what Nico tells his doctor.
Nico lets the boy have the credit, squares it with Aldo, finds the job was a trap either way, and carries the cake to the dinner. Enzo keeps Nico's secret, or seems to.

## Story reasons
- The tail and the stake-out: Nico cannot ask, because asking tells Sergio he knows.
- The therapy choice: this is the one decision in the episode that is Nico's alone, so it is the one that changes a mission.
- The heist: it is the price Sergio named, and Nico doing it himself is how he keeps both the boy and the money out of Aldo's hands.
- The escort: it is the bill for the heist arriving at the door of the one person who did nothing.
- The negotiation: Aldo and Nico are the only two grown-ups in the story, and it pays off the man in a nice tie from Camera Shy.
- The cake: a boss making a captain carry a cake is a message, and the cargo meter is the joke.
