# Episode 27: Clean Money

## Arc
Episode 26 ended with Gina finding the bank book: "So we pay people now not to talk about us." She answers it her own way, with her own savings: she buys a third of Bean Scene, the coffee bar, from its owner Rosa Kemp. Something that is hers, and clean.
On her first morning two brothers from over the bridge, Cal and Ozzie Dunleavy, come in for "Mr. Bellacosta's envelope". Nobody of Nico's has ever collected there. Somebody is using his name, in his wife's shop.
A tail, a stake-out with a long lens and a getaway, Dr. Vos, a little book lifted out of a pickup at Big Mo's, a race back to the shop, and a round to give the money back, which is the strangest job Nico's crew has ever done. At home, Gina wants to know whose money it was.

## Story reasons
- Silent Partner: Gina bought her way into something clean after the bank book. Nico follows the envelope instead of making a scene, because the shop is hers and the morning is hers.
- The Dunleavy Boys: before Nico does anything to two men using his name, he needs to know whether anybody stands behind them. The photograph is proof for Uncle Sergio; the flash is why they run.
- Her Own Money: the wife's doubts against the business: does Nico protect her shop the way he protects everything (quietly, his way), or does he let her be a partner in knowing?
- The Little Book: the book is the brothers' whole business. Without it they cannot remember who pays, and Nico can prove they used his name.
- Opening Hours: a crew that loses its book goes for the one shop it knows is his. Fists only, because it is Gina's shop and Rosa's window.
- Clean Money: family vs business in one round: giving the money back makes the street know whose name it is, and makes Sergio ask what kind of boss gives money back. Gina's kitchen settles the rest.
