# Episode 24: The Weekly Column

## Arc
Episode 23 ended with Augie writing the fed a boring napkin every Friday, and the table counting Enzo's quiet week. This Friday the lifebuoy box has an answer in it: Pembrook liked the writing, and wants to know "who the tall one is at Mancuso's".
Augie is flattered, Enzo wants the column for himself (a writer for the government is nearly a made man, to Enzo), and Nico has to wonder whether a man who enjoys writing to the fed this much might one day write him something true.
Dr. Vos helps Nico decide who writes the next napkin (Enzo, or Augie). The crew gives the fed a "tall one" he can photograph (a shop dummy in a suit), and then finds out Aldo Ferro's runner has been reading the box too. The Pelican table, and Gina, decide what the column says from now on.

## Story reasons
- Letters Page: until now the box went one way. A reply means the fed is talking back, and the crew must know what he asked before he asks anybody else.
- Fan Mail: the possible informant this week is a friend who is enjoying himself. Nico follows before he accuses, because last time following was what showed him the truth.
- Writer's Block: the nephew who wants to be made sees the column as a promotion. Dr. Vos makes Nico choose between trusting Enzo's words (family) and the safe hand (business).
- The Tall One: the fed asked a question; the cheapest true-looking answer is a dummy he can photograph from his car. Careful driving because a dummy with no head answers nothing.
- Wrong Box: a rival crew reading the fed's mail is the rival crew finding out what the fed knows about them. A chase, because Hobie has the napkin and a car.
- The Weekly Column: the table settles Aldo and the pen (business); the kitchen settles a napkin Gina read (family), and the wife's doubts get an answer she likes, for once.
