# Episode Eighteen: Question Marks

## Arc
Episode Seventeen ended with Marco beaten at the table. Under his mattress, beside the negatives, Nico also took a list: names from his own crew, each with a question mark, and a telephone number at the bottom.
Nico asks the men on the list what Marco asked them, rings the number, and gets a federal switchboard: the man who called Enzo a rat was ringing Agent Pembrook himself. A long-lens picture on the pier proves it.
Dr. Vos helps him choose between Aldo's way (respect: let the uncle see it) and business (feed the rat a story). Marco runs, Nico brings him back in one piece, and this time Gina hears it first, before any uncle does.

## Story reasons
- The rounds: the list is a list of questions already asked. The only way to know what Marco was collecting is to ask the men he asked, quietly, before any of them wonders why his name is on a list.
- The stake-out: Pembrook lost his Thursday with Enzo. A man like that fills the slot. Nico sits where he can see the pier and waits, because a picture is the only proof Aldo's family would accept after last week.
- The therapy choice: the episode's question is whose job it is to punish a rat who is somebody else's nephew. Respect leaves it to his uncle; business makes the rat useful. Both protect Enzo, and the session is where Nico picks.
- Bait: Aldo's line throws Nico's own words at the table back at him, so he must see it himself (an escort against the clock). The deal path is a lie planted the way Marco planted envelopes, past the same kind of lookout.
- Last Ride: a rat who is found out runs to the only man who would hide him. A chase, not a killing, because Aldo asked for him back "in one piece; the car I do not care about".
- First to Know: Gina's line at the end of Seventeen. The wife's doubt closes the episode as it did Fifteen to Seventeen, and the table ends Marco's story with a sentence Aldo picks, not a gun.
