# Episode 25: Situations Vacant

## Arc
Episode 24 ended with Hobie Lusk, Aldo Ferro's driver, caught reading the lifebuoy box, and the table writing its napkins "for two readers". On Monday Aldo fires Hobie for panicking, and Lyle Teague comes to Mancuso's to take his car keys back, with two men to add interest. In front of everybody, Hobie asks Nico for a job.
Enzo wants him: a man of his own would make Enzo nearly made. Nico has to wonder whether a man who read the fed's mail for Aldo is now being sent to read Nico's.
A trial run with a grey sedan behind it, Dr. Vos, a stake-out that shows who is really talking to the fed (Lyle, who counts for himself first), a lift of Lyle's ledger at the diner, and a walk round the pond with Aldo where Nico trades it. At home, Gina has already met Hobie.

## Story reasons
- Help Wanted: a rival crew putting a man out on the street outside Nico's shop is a message to Nico. Fists, because Nico wants it remembered, not reported.
- Trial Run: before he trusts a man who read other people's mail, Nico watches what he does with his own crew's envelopes. The tail on them raises the question of who sent whom.
- Probation: the nephew who wants to be made wants a man under him. Dr. Vos makes Nico choose between letting Enzo be trusted with a person (family) and the safe move (business).
- The Grey Sedan: following the follower is the only way to know if Hobie is the informant. The answer is somebody else.
- Counts for Himself: Lyle skims Aldo's money and sells the fed what he knows. His ledger proves both, and it is worth more than any threat.
- Situations Vacant: the trade settles Hobie and Aldo (business); Gina and the flowers settle whether Nico tells her who comes to the house (the wife's doubts, family).
