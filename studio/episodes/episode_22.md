# Episode 22: The Fern Man

## Arc
Episode 21 ended with Agent Pembrook reading a fake notebook ("N.B. watered the fern"), and the real one saying "Ask about the wife." Now the fed is coming to see the fern for himself, and so Nico has to own one.
Pembrook goes round Kilbane and buys a lemon cake from Gina at the church bake sale. What he really asks her about is Enzo, who has been telling the bar his name is going in the book.
Dr. Vos helps Nico choose between trusting Gina with the fed (calm) and sending her away (deal). Then Enzo sets off to "talk" to the fed with a bat, Nico has to run his nephew off the road, and the Pelican table decides what a nephew who cannot wait is good for.

## Story reasons
- Fern Season: Augie's fake notebook only works if it is true. A fed who is told that Nico watered a fern will drive past to see it, so the fern has to get home in one piece and be standing in the window.
- Lemon Cake: "Ask about the wife" was the last line of the real notebook, and Pembrook is doing it himself now. Nico cannot walk up to his own wife's table while the fed is at it, so he listens. And a fed who has been made a fool of follows the man he blames.
- Third Party: the wife's doubts against business. Gina is now part of it whether Nico likes it or not, and he has to decide whether that makes her a partner or a risk.
- The Second Cup: calm trusts her: she does it her way, and he can only watch. Deal is protective, and it costs her a weekend and him a clock. Either way the fed's real interest comes out: Enzo's mouth.
- Big Talk: the nephew who wants to be made thinks hurting a fed is how you prove it. Stopping him is the most family thing Nico does all episode, and he does it with a bumper.
- The Fern Man: the table settles Enzo (business), and the kitchen settles the fern (family). The comedy is that the fern is the only honest thing in the house.
