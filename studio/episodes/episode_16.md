# Episode Sixteen: The Brown Sedan

## Arc
Episode Fifteen ended with Uncle Sergio putting Enzo's name forward, and Gina asking Nico whether he wanted the boy to become him.
Two days before Sunday, Aldo Ferro rings: Marco has photographs of Enzo getting out of a fed's brown sedan at the pier. Is the boy talking?
Nico finds out that Agent Pembrook has been squeezing Enzo over the Haskell oil every Thursday, and that Enzo has said no every time. Nico takes the squeeze away, and Enzo gets his button. Gina asks what it cost.

## Story reasons
- The chase: the photographs are the danger, not the man who took them. Rudy is a wedding photographer who took fifty dollars. Fists only, because Rudy has helped Nico before (Episodes Ten and Twelve).
- The eavesdrop: Nico cannot ask Enzo without telling him he is suspected, two days before his button. Listening first is how a careful boss finds out. Hearing Enzo say no is the turn of the episode.
- The therapy choice: the episode's question is trust. Dr. Vos points out that Nico listens from a distance to everybody he loves. Asking or testing is his answer, and it changes the next mission.
- The test tail: if he tests the boy, he has to watch where the envelope goes. Enzo driving straight to Sergio is the proof, and Enzo knowing he was followed is the price.
- The timed and careful drives: the fed's hook is the stolen pickup from Episode Fifteen. If the owner says it was lent, the hook is gone. Haskell is old and has a bad back, so the drive has to be gentle, and the fed only waits until noon.
- The shake: the fed would love a picture of a ceremony. Losing him is the last thing Nico can do for the boy before Sunday.
- Gina: the wife's doubt closes the episode, as it closed Fifteen. The boy got what he wanted by saying no to a fed, and she asks how many more times he will have to.
