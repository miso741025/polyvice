# Episode 21: Each Way

## Arc
Episode Twenty ended with Dusty Kilbane beaten at the Pelican table over a biscuit tin of children's lunch money. Three weeks later he pays Augie early, and with a smile, and that worries Nico more than if he had not paid at all.
Nico tails him to the pier, where he talks to Agent Pembrook, the man from the brown sedan. Around town, Kilbane has been asking who sits where at the Pelican.
Dr. Vos helps Nico choose between asking Kilbane straight (calm) and scaring him (deal). Kilbane admits he sells the fed a notebook every Friday. Nico swaps it for one Augie wrote, and the table decides how a man who bets each way gets paid.

## Story reasons
- Paid Early: a beaten man who pays early is a man who has found a new friend. Nico follows rather than asks, because he has to know who the friend is first.
- Asking Around: a possible informant is the oldest fear in the business. Before Nico accuses anybody, he finds out what Kilbane wanted to know: who sits where, and on which night.
- Win or Place: Kilbane bets each way, on the fed and on Nico, and Nico has to decide which way to bet on Kilbane. Family against business again: Uncle Sergio was at that table.
- Round the Pond: the calm road is a slow walk, because a man walking beside you cannot look you in the eye and lie for long. The deal road is a chase, because a man who runs has something to say.
- Two Notebooks: killing a talker brings more raincoats. Changing what he says is quieter. The clock is Pembrook's coffee.
- Each Way: the table settles the talker (business). The kitchen settles the wife's doubts (family). The notebook is funny until it is about Gina.
