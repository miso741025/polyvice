# Episode Seventeen: A Nice One

## Arc
Episode Sixteen ended with Enzo made, and Rudy Vance admitting that Marco kept one print of the pier photograph.
A week later, copies of it turn up in envelopes all over Nico's crew: Marco, sore about the book, is making the new made man look like a rat.
Nico collects every copy, takes the original out of Marco's motel room, and lets Aldo Ferro's nephew lose the argument at the table. Then Gina gets the photograph of Enzo she asked for: a nice one.

## Story reasons
- The rounds: Rudy said one copy was left. A spiteful man with one print makes more. Collecting them before the captains open them is what a careful boss does the week after a button; the chase is the man caught doing it, and fists only because Wes is a lookout, not an enemy.
- The tail: nobody knows where Marco sleeps since Nico took the pickup back from the motor lodge. Following him from his coffee is the only way to find the original without asking his uncle.
- The therapy choice: the episode's question is what a picture is worth. Burning it keeps Enzo's Thursdays a secret; showing it to Sergio makes the truth the family's, not Marco's. Both are Nico's way of protecting the boy, and the session is where he picks one.
- Room Four: the original and the negatives are the last of it. Same motor lodge, same lookout trick as Load Out, and Marco cannot complain to anybody about a room he is hiding in.
- The table: Marco loses because he has nothing left to show, or because Sergio has already seen it and does not care. A sit-down, not a fight, because Aldo is the one who asked, and Nico wins it by talking.
- A Nice One: Gina's line at the end of Sixteen. The family album is the opposite of the pier photograph, and the wife's doubt closes the episode as it closed Fifteen and Sixteen.
