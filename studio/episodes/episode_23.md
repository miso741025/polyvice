# Episode 23: The Night Barman

## Arc
Episode 22 ended with Enzo told to keep his mouth shut, and Pembrook still fishing for him. Enzo has said nothing all week, and yet whatever is said at the bar on a Tuesday, the fed knows on Wednesday.
The leak is Wendell Crisp, Bianca's new night barman, who writes what he overhears on napkins and sells them to Pembrook at twenty dollars each. Nico follows him, and finds the little red book in his glove box.
Dr. Vos helps Nico choose between letting Enzo handle his own leak with words (calm) and putting the barman on the last bus out of town (deal). Either way the crew writes the fed a very boring napkin, and the Pelican table and Gina have the last word.

## Story reasons
- Twenty a Napkin: Enzo was told to keep quiet, and did; so if the fed still knows things, somebody else is talking. Nico follows before he accuses, because the last time it was a man he had beaten at cards.
- The Glove Box: a photograph shows a meeting, not what was sold. The book shows what was sold, and where the next drop is. The patrol car is the bar's street at one in the morning.
- Small Print: the possible informant is not family, which makes him easy to hurt. Dr. Vos makes Nico say whether that is a reason to, and whether his nephew is ready to handle a problem with his mouth instead of a bat.
- A Word with Wendell: calm is Enzo's test as a man who wants to be made: a sit-down where saying less wins. Deal is Nico protecting the family the old way, and it costs a frightened barman his town.
- The Lifebuoy: killing a leak brings more raincoats; feeding it is quieter. The napkin says Enzo said nothing, which is finally true.
- The Night Barman: the table settles the barman and the nephew (business); the kitchen settles a napkin in a coat pocket (family), and the wife's doubts get the truth for once.
